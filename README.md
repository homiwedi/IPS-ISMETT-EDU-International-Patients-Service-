# IPS-ISMETT-EDU-International-Patients-Service-
Registration Check

A patient portal for UPMC ISMETT's International Patients Service. Patients verify their identity with their full name and MRN number, then view their medical invoice, including diagnosis, planned procedure, and cost summary, with an option to print or save it as a PDF.

The site is implemented in `index.html`. Patient photos referenced from the invoice page live in `assets/`.
